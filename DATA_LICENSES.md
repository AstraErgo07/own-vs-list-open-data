## JD Worldwide Open Platform category fee standard

- **Official public rule page.**
  https://jdw-rule.jd.hk/detail?ruleId=950583665543483392
- **What it is.** The published operation-support service fee (category
  commission), transaction service fee (0.90% uniform for POP merchants
  from 1 April 2023), and seller deposits, by JD's own first-, second- and
  third-level category names. This is a platform rule page, not a product
  listing.
- **Scope.** JD Worldwide (cross-border). A domestic JD.com POP card may
  differ and was not independently available as a public page without a
  merchant login. That is disclosed in the app and the README.
- **Label.** Published (JD Worldwide Open Platform category fee standard,
  rule 950583665543483392).

## Datasets no longer used

The Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) and
UCI Online Retail II (Chen, D., 2012, CC BY 4.0) were used in an earlier
version and have been **removed**. They were a different market, dated,
and (in UCI's case) a wholesale-priced 1P basis that was not credible.
No Olist or UCI files are in this repository.
