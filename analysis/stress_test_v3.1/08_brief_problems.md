# What in the brief does not make sense

Listed so it can be passed on. These are problems with the brief, not
with the model.

1. **Task 6 is a rebuild, not a review.** Adding price-band
   distributions, Pareto SKU curves and three new categories changes
   the model. Mixing those results into the v3.1 verdict tables would
   test a model that was not the one published. Task 6 is treated as
   v3.2 work and is kept out of the v3.1 tables.

2. **Task 4 compares unlike things.** Amazon's 61% is a worldwide
   share of paid units across all categories (Q4 2025 earnings,
   excluding Whole Foods). B is five UK categories, measured by
   assumed sales. A gap will appear whether or not B is wrong. The
   brief's request to "state the gap" and "say whether B is
   plausibly miscalibrated" is therefore too strong. This stress test
   reports only whether the gap points in a plausible direction.

3. **The Monte Carlo "fragile" flag is a function of the ranges.**
   Drawing uniformly across ranges that were chosen in the brief
   (or, for coverage, reset by Task 0) makes "no verdict exceeds 60%"
   depend on those ranges. Every Monte Carlo result is printed next
   to the ranges that produced it. The 60% cut itself is sharp:
   laundry at B is 60.29% Keep (not fragile) and pushchairs at A,
   unit, is 58.62% Bring (fragile).

4. **"The four tests" lists five.** Contribution versus take,
   inventory liquidity, GMROI, demand predictability, and the
   strategic tilt. Attribution uses all five.

5. **The brief's 0–30% entrant coverage range assumes a marketplace
   that Task 0 does not find.** Thirty percent would be a material
   seller base. The evidence is a 1P-only launch, a planned
   invitation-only curated test in H2 2026, "no third-party listings"
   as of September 2026 (LockerRules, 6 October), and no published
   coverage share. This stress test uses 0–10% as an
   evidence-informed range (10% is a generous Assumption upper bound
   for a curated test, not a published figure) and reports 0–100%
   only to locate the exact 100%-1P break.

6. **Haircut, variable-share and introductory commission are inert
   at B** under v3.1 construction (haircut interpolates to 0;
   fulfilment is fully loaded; commission is Amazon referral). Asking
   for flip points on those at B is asking for a result the model
   cannot produce. They are reported as inert and are not drawn in
   the B Monte Carlo.
