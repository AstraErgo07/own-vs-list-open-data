## What this means for 0% entrant seller coverage

- **At launch (March 2026), 0% is supported.** Joybuy was 1P only.
- **As of the latest dated consumer-facing description (LockerRules,
  6 October 2026, stating the September 2026 position), there were
  "no third-party listings."** That is still consistent with 0%
  coverage on the shopper site, but it is a third-party description,
  not a Joybuy disclosure, and it is more than a month old by this
  check.
- **By mid-November 2026 a thin invitation-only test may be under
  way.** Operator One's 24 September wording ("started testing") and
  the partner portal's Seller route are consistent with a curated
  test. How many listings, in which categories, and what share of
  any sub-category range they can actually supply, are all
  **UNVERIFIED**. No published source supports a specific non-zero
  coverage number.
- **The v3.1 default of 0% is therefore still the only evidenced
  launch-day value, and it is not contradicted by a published
  coverage figure.** It is no longer strictly proven for 13 November
  2026. The honest wording is: 0% is an Assumption, evidenced at
  launch and not replaced by a later published share.

## Evidence-based range used in this stress test

The original brief suggested drawing entrant coverage uniformly from
0% to 30%. That 30% upper bound is **not supported by the evidence**.
Thirty percent would be a material seller base, not a "trusted brands"
curated test, and no source gives anything like that share.

This stress test therefore uses an **evidence-informed entrant
coverage range of 0% to 10%** for one-at-a-time flips (inside the
range) and for the Monte Carlo:

- **0%** is the evidenced launch state and the v3.1 default.
- **10%** is a generous Assumption upper bound for an invitation-only
  curated test, not a published figure. It sits inside v3.1's already
  stated "small early 3P presence" sensitivity (5–15%). It is labelled
  Assumption.
- **30% is reported only as a 'beyond the evidence' sensitivity**, so
  the exact coverage at which the entrant stops being 100% 1P can
  still be stated if it lies above 10%.
