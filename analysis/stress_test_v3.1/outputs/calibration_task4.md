Amazon has disclosed that third-party sellers accounted for 61% of worldwide paid units (Q4 2025) (Supplemental Financial Information and Business Metrics: 'WW seller unit mix — % of WW paid units' was 61% in Q4 2025 (Q1 2025: 61%; Q2 2025: 62%; Q3 2025: 62%). Footnote: excludes the impact of Whole Foods Market. Paid units are physical and digital units sold (net of returns and cancellations) by Amazon and by sellers in its stores.; https://ir.aboutamazon.com/news-release/news-release-details/2026/Amazon-com-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-Results/). That is worldwide paid units, excluding Whole Foods, across Amazon's full category mix. It is not a UK sales share, and it is not a five-category share.

Under v3.1 defaults, established (B) mid-band 1P is 25% of the 20 sub-categories and 5.6% of modelled mid-band sales (Assumption: ex-VAT price × assumed weekly units, five UK categories only).

A naive subtraction — 39% 1P units (implied by 61% 3P) versus 5.6% 1P modelled sales — is a comparison of unlike things: geography, unit versus sales, and category mix all differ. This stress test does not treat that gap as a hard miscalibration.

Price-mix illustration (Assumption, not an Amazon disclosure). If 1P units sold at the same average price as 3P units, 39% of units would be 39% of sales. If 1P units sold at half the 3P average price, 1P sales share would be 24.2%. If 1P units sold at twice the 3P average, 1P sales share would be 56.1%. In this model's five categories, 1P recommendations sit in lower-priced beauty and baby care, so a sales share below a unit share is the direction the model's own price mix produces — but the model's 6% sales share is still a long way below even the half-price illustration (24.2%). That points in the direction of 'B is 1P-light relative to Amazon's worldwide unit mix', for these five categories. It does not prove B is wrong, because Amazon's 61% includes categories this model does not (for example grocery, media, and consumables) and is not a UK sales figure. The parameters that pull B's 1P sales share down in this model are a rich 3P take (Amazon UK referral grossed up for VAT, plus a 3% ad take and a warehouse margin), thin fully loaded contribution on durables (Currys 18.3% after 12% fulfilment) and fashion (Zalando 23% fulfilment), and 90% seller coverage. Those are not tuned here.
